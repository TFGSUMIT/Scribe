# ADR-003 — Adopt Discord as the Primary Platform

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context

Scribe aims to assist tabletop RPG groups during online sessions.

The majority of online RPG communities already use Discord for voice communication.

Supporting multiple platforms from the beginning would significantly increase project complexity.

## Decision

Discord will be the only supported platform during the initial releases.

Support for additional platforms may be evaluated after version 1.0.

## Alternatives Considered

### Multi-platform Support

Platforms:

- Discord
- TeamSpeak
- Guilded
- Telegram
- Zoom

Pros

- Larger audience

Cons

- Increased complexity
- Higher maintenance cost

## Consequences

Positive

- Faster development
- Better user experience
- Simpler architecture

Negative

- Smaller initial audience
