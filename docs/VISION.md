# Vision

Every tabletop RPG campaign tells a unique story.

Unfortunately, many memorable moments are lost after the session ends.

Players forget names.

Dungeon Masters forget NPC voices.

Entire adventures disappear over time.

Scribe exists to solve this problem.

Rather than recording audio, Scribe creates a living memory of the campaign.

It understands context, identifies speakers, organizes information and transforms hours of conversation into structured knowledge.

The objective is simple:

Never lose your story again.
