# Product

## Product Name

Scribe

## Tagline

**The Memory of Your Adventure.**

## Elevator Pitch

Scribe is an AI-powered Discord companion that listens, understands and remembers every chapter of your tabletop RPG campaign.

Unlike traditional transcription bots, Scribe transforms voice conversations into structured campaign knowledge, generating summaries, tracking NPCs, quests, locations and important events automatically.

## Mission

Capture every word.
Preserve every adventure.

## Vision

Become the definitive AI companion for tabletop RPG campaigns on Discord.

## Core Values

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Privacy First
- AI as an assistant, never the protagonist
- Preserve stories, not just conversations

## Target Audience

- Dungeon Masters
- RPG Groups
- D&D
- Pathfinder
- Call of Cthulhu
- Tormenta20
- Ordem Paranormal
- Any tabletop RPG played on Discord

## Non Goals

Scribe is NOT:

- A virtual tabletop
- A dice bot
- A music bot
- A moderation bot
- A character sheet manager
